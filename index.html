Questions, answered

What is OKLiv?

Is OKLiv only for Slavic users?

No. OKLiv is for anyone curious about Slavic culture and open to meeting people through music, food, language, style and everyday life.

Can I message anyone directly?

Private chat opens after you follow each other or become friends. This helps keep conversations relevant, mutual and respectful.

What can I do during a live?

You can watch creators, join the discussion, react in real time and send in-app gifts while the host is live.

What is the AI Slavic Makeup feature?

It is a playful creative tool that generates stylized makeup ideas inspired by regional Slavic aesthetics and lets you share the result with the community.
